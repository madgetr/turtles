🐢 Turtle Yard – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Turtle Yard is a pet turtle game for kids and families. We built it to be safe for children: it does not ask for, keep or sell any personal information.

What the game stores

Your turtles, yards, stars and collection are saved only on your own device, using the device's local storage. This information stays on the device and we cannot see it, except for the parts a player chooses to show a friend (see Visiting friends). Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's data for the web version) deletes it.

Visiting friends (optional)

Players can visit each other's yards and give turtles to friends. Nothing is shared unless a player uses this, and every visit needs a "yes" from the player whose yard it is.

  • How it works: the game connects to a small relay server (run on Cloudflare) that passes messages between the two games. The server does not save them.
  • What is sent: a random code the game makes for the device (not linked to who you are), and, only during a visit or gift the player agreed to, the yard being visited (its name, its turtles and their names, and the yard layout) or the one turtle being given.
  • What is not sent: no names of people, no messages or chat, no photos, no location. The names players give their friends stay on their own device.
  • Network data: like any internet connection, the relay sees the device's IP address while it is connected. It is used only to deliver messages and to stop people guessing visit codes, and is not stored.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, names, email addresses or phone numbers are asked for.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking identifiers are used.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party services are included.
  • No in-app purchases or payments.
  • No chat or messages between players, and no contact with strangers: a visit only happens when the host says yes.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Names that players type for their turtles and yards stay on their device.

Permissions

The Android app asks for no special permissions. It uses vibration for small haptic effects, and the standard internet permission that Android web-based apps include. The internet is only used for the optional Visiting friends feature.

Children

Turtle Yard is designed for children and families and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not keep any information about players, so there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete. Parents can remove friends inside the game (👫 Friends, then ✕), or simply not use the Friends feature.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
