🐢 Turtle Yard – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Turtle Yard is a pet turtle game for kids and families. We built it to be safe for children: it does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your turtles, yards, stars and collection are saved only on your own device, using the device's local storage. This information never leaves the device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's data for the web version) deletes it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, names, email addresses or phone numbers are asked for.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking identifiers are used.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party services are included.
  • No in-app purchases or payments.
  • No chat, sharing or contact with other players.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Names that players type for their turtles and yards stay on their device.

Permissions

The Android app asks for no special permissions. It uses vibration for small haptic effects, and the standard internet permission that Android web-based apps include; the game does not send or receive any player data over the internet.

Children

Turtle Yard is designed for children and families and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the developer using the email address on the Turtle Yard listing in the Google Play Store, or open an issue at github.com/madgetr/turtles.
